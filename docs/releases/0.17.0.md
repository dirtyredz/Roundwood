# Roundwood 0.17.0 (NeoForge 1.21.1) - first build of the NeoForge line

Tag `neoforge-1.21.1-v0.17.0`, jar `roundwood-1.21.1-neoforge-0.17.0.jar`. Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge
21.1.265 or newer 21.1, Java 21. Optional viewers: JEI 19.70.0.464, EMI 1.1.24+1.21.1. Same content,
ids and behaviour as Forge 0.17.0 (notes below); the Forge 1.20.1 line continues on `main`.

- Source: branch start `4a3bdfb` (Forge 0.17.0 `4610a4b` plus structure cleanups and docs).
- Worlds from Forge 0.13.3 (the pack, jar SHA-256 `150874f1…f206e4`) and 0.17.0 load after Minecraft's
  conversion with Roundwood installed: every block, item and block-entity id is kept, saved looks, attachments,
  glass, hardware, fitted frames and camouflage arrive as saved, and the 0.13.3 archway and crossed-window
  migrations run as before. Evidence: [save compatibility](../SAVE-COMPATIBILITY.md).
- Item payloads live in `minecraft:custom_data` (unchanged shapes); names, enchantments and placed contents
  use their native components. A camouflage refund from 1.20.1 is converted once; one naming a missing item
  stays locked on the door (no edit, carried on the door's drop) instead of disappearing.
- Projected door use is the `roundwood:door_use` payload, protocol 2: a Forge 0.17.0 client cannot join a
  NeoForge server or the other way round (they are different Minecraft versions anyway).
- Not yet: the whole-pack world conversion and restart, which the Evil Eye pack session runs.

## Content (Forge 0.17.0 notes)


- Connected arch render edges and shared mesh vertices prevent the stepped-edge gaps created by
  independent face offsets. Donor textures keep authored coordinates; logical placement stays exact.
- Cached settled open-door selection geometry and donor hardware targets. Repeated render frames
  reuse projected rays and hardware previews within a tick; clicks still validate the live world.

- Cached the surviving animated leaf's inputs, model locations and sweep bounds; reloads and finish
  changes invalidate its materials. Both leaves, depth offsets, reversals and off-screen rendering remain.

- Static fitted/standalone frames, casings, camouflage and window attachments use cached chunk geometry with immutable data.
  Owner screenshots approved owning-cell shading for the proud casing, front door casing and
  camouflaged corner, so the old static renderers and their comparison option are removed. Leaves
  still animate through their BER.

- Added Frameless Window Pane: full 16x16 glazing with existing tint, waterlogging,
  floor/ceiling/depth placement and optional Cross Beams. Craft one from one glass pane.
  It has no wooden border, perimeter trim or sill, and unsupported edits are free.
- Fitted round arches and lower-corner camouflage now work in either order. Upper fill
  is hidden by the arch; the existing camouflage donor and single refund are preserved.
- Every knob is centred one block above the floor. Spear-ended hinges meet the actual
  leaf edge, with a three-pixel root, tapered shaft/tip and half-pixel face projection.
  Paired upper hinges sit below their crowns.
- Raised the 2x3 top's spring by eight pixels. Matching 3x3/3x4 doors now reach their
  assembly tops. Existing IDs, footprints, saved cells and fitted offsets are preserved.
- Separated door/arch render planes throughout swing, including both casing faces and
  standalone arches. Logical geometry and accepted swing clipping are unchanged.
- Added cosmetic finishes from iron, gold, diamond, emerald and netherite blocks;
  all four full copper stages (waxed donors share unwaxed looks); and quartz block,
  smooth quartz, chiseled quartz, quartz bricks and quartz pillar. Actual donor textures
  tile at block scale, follow resource packs, never oxidize and do not accept dye.
  Cut copper, stairs and slabs are excluded.
- Ordinary-click hinges or knobs with a supported donor to style that slot across the
  assembly. Either face/leaf and either hand work; sneaking has the same result.
  The preview uses the server's endpoint proposal. Blocked, stale or moving requests
  cannot charge or toggle; wait for settling (eight ticks for a full swing).
- One donor pays for a changed hardware slot; unchanged and creative edits are free.
  Door items preserve both slots through pick, harvest, replacement and arch fitting,
  with no raw donor refund. Unknown hardware payloads remain opaque and cannot be
  edited or placed lossily.
- Added bounded showcase examples for centre glazing, residual camouflage, raised
  crowns and independent hardware, plus all 14 distinct surface finishes on the wall.
  The advertised item count is 29; the thirteen retired crossed IDs are unchanged.

Owner trial checklist:

- Inspect the reported stepped arch, including wood and masonry, full/thin depths and fitted casings.
  Compare settled-open versus closed FPS,
  then hold a donor while aiming at doors and arches. Move the camera, swap hands/donors, edit a
  casing, place a nearer obstruction, reverse a swing and reload resources to check invalidation.

- Recheck a proud standalone casing, front door casing and camouflaged round-door corner. Include
  all door depths, AO on/off, and Forge's experimental lighting pipeline on/off.
- Check proud bands at x/z and y section edges, non-anchor mining cracks, and appearance particles.
  Bare animated leaves still have no static chunk geometry for cracks.
- Restyle/remove casings and sills, fit/break/refit arches, reload resources with F3+T, and leave/return
  to the chunks. Confirm no stale pieces or double drawing. Test with and without Embeddium.
- Compare warmed showcase FPS/frame times at the identical viewpoint. Headless timings do not
  establish an FPS improvement.

- Recreate both feedback screenshots; repeatedly open/close from both sides.
- Inspect all four designs, both hinges, front/middle/back depths and compatible full/thin arches.
- Check strap contact, knob height, paired crown height, and absence of flicker during reversal.
- Exercise both camouflage orders and an open projected knob; try nearer walls and moving clicks.
- Inspect donor textures, inventory icons, resource reload and a second client's updates.
- Inspect a frameless centre pane in a larger window for visible seams.

Existing saves adopt the new geometry without block replacement. No visible client or full-pack
boot is part of agent verification; these visual checks remain for the owner.

- Consolidated fitted arch bodies and casings into merged surfaces, preserving donor tiling,
  masonry joints and render-plane separation. The captured showcase fitted workload now uses
  13,410 watertight quads instead of 25,686 with the same 0.17.0 crown. Client FPS remains an owner check.
- Extracted fitted-frame surfaces and cached models into DoorArchModels with a typed cache key.
  The 135-case corpus pins 13,410 conforming quads through the chunk collector.
