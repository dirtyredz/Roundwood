# Roundwood 0.16.0 (Forge 1.20.1) - trial

- Fit any Roundwood door into its matching arch top with an ordinary click, in either
  placement order. Arched 1x2 uses Top 1x1, Round 2x2 uses Top 1x2, and both paired
  doors use Top 2x3 (at y+1 for 3x3, y+2 for 3x4).
- Fitted leaves preserve aimed FRONT/MIDDLE/BACK depth. Ground placement automatically
  finds a compatible top above; direct top clicks and reverse-order fitting still work.
  The preview shares the server proposal. Thin fits refuse only an equal closed door/slab
  plane, shown in red, without moving either component. Full tops retain standalone geometry;
  the swinging leaf may clip through full or thin frames.
- Visible middle and free-edge clicks toggle fitted leaves even when the ray passes outside
  every owned cell. Nearer frames and blocks still occlude the leaf.
- Added thin 3/16 versions of all four modular arch products, at FRONT/MIDDLE/BACK.
  Craft one full piece alone into one matching thin piece, preserving its appearance
  and supported stack metadata. Conversion is one-way; unknown payloads refuse it.
- Door, frame and their casing faces retain independent wood, paint or masonry looks.
  Frame hits edit/pick the arch; leaf hits edit/pick/toggle the door. Breaking any owned
  cell returns each component once under the usual survival, creative and explosion rules.
- Both placement orders validate every cell and roll back full state/NBT on refusal or
  cancellation. Existing doors, arches and retired migration targets stay intact until
  explicitly fitted. Arched doors now support depth targeting, defaulting old states to FRONT.
- Added labelled showcase examples for every fitted size and eligible frame depth, plus
  all standalone thin depths. The advertised item count is 28.

Vanilla and other-mod doors keep separate-cell placement. Keyhole shoulders are standalone
pieces and cannot become fitted tops. Unknown saved frame data remains opaque and read-only;
if harvested, it stays on the door item and placement refuses to discard it.

Owner checks: aim at each floor depth band beneath all four top sizes, then try direct
arch clicks and both placement orders. Check thin equal-plane refusals against previews.
Click the middle and free edge from both sides, including open leaves viewed from outside
the doorway; verify a nearer frame/wall blocks clicks. Open,
close and reverse both hinges while inspecting hardware and the full-arch faces. Inspect all
thin depths and mirrored shoulders, wood/paint/masonry and independently styled casings.
Check redstone, walking clearance, save/reload and separate drops in the showcase.
No visible client was launched for this change.
