# Roundwood 0.14.0 (Forge 1.20.1) — in development

- Includes the 0.13.4 door fix: clicking an open round door's projected free edge
  closes its own door through server-validated reach, obstruction and permission
  checks. Collision and swing geometry are unchanged. Both ends need the update.
- Added the geometry, assembly and placement foundations for three semicircular
  tops (height x width 1x1, 1x2, 2x3) and a mirrored keyhole shoulder. Pieces are dry,
  require no supporting floor, and reserve their complete footprints. Tops preserve
  non-colliding crown clearance; shoulders retain only the old upper transition.
- Existing archways retain their current behaviour. New products remain outside
  advertised lists until the content batch supplies their models, recipes and names.

Rendering, attachments, recipes and showcase notes will be completed with those
implementation batches. This intermediate branch is not a finished feature release.
