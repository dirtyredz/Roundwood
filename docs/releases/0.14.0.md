# Roundwood 0.14.0 (Forge 1.20.1) - trial

- Fixed clicks on the projected free edge of open round and arched doors. Server validation keeps
  the real aimed point and checks reach, obstruction, assembly and permissions before normal use.
- Replaced the two old archways with four advertised products: Arch Top 1x1, 1x2 and 2x3
  (height x width), and a LEFT/RIGHT Keyhole Shoulder. Tops have empty collision; the large top
  owns all six cells. Dry placement, targeting previews, independent looks and front/back casings
  share the existing assembly/attachment services. Masonry has contour joints and centred keystones.
- Owner feedback: the 1x1 now contains only upper door corners (radius 8, spring y=8).
  The 1x2/2x3 openings use full half-width radii 16/24, removing the spring ledge beside
  full blocks. Shoulder bases extend to the floor at six pixels wide. Casings, outlines,
  collision, masonry and icons follow these profiles.
- Old placed ordinary arches become an upper-row 1x2 top with air below. Keyholes also get two
  full-height lower shoulders. The crown widens from radius 14 to 16; the keyhole lower
  shape is preserved. Body looks and facing survive; both casings move to the top in their own looks.
  Migration is atomic and repeat-safe. Unknown trim versions defer; unloaded/damaged assemblies
  follow the existing conservative integrity policy.
- Old stacks become one top per item, plus two shoulders per keyhole item. Loaded holders preserve
  looks and source metadata; excess shoulders drop at the holder. Failed overflow leaves the source
  untouched. Old IDs remain non-placeable migration sources with no recipes or advertised entries.
- Four new recipes replace the old two; creative, EMI and JEI show 22 active products. The showcase
  includes every top, paired shoulders, ordinary/keyhole/taller compositions and casing/masonry examples.

Owner trial: load a world saved in 0.13.x that has archways, inspect positions/looks/casings and
old chest/player stacks, then save/reload to check idempotence. Check overflow with a full inventory.
Try all tops/shoulder sides and facings, six-cell obstruction, walking clearance, cherry/paint/all
four masonry looks, front/back trims, icons, resource reload and chunk-edge rendering. Check the
showcase and recipe viewers. Close open doors from their free edges, both hinges and both sides,
including a second client. Both clients and server need the updated mod.
