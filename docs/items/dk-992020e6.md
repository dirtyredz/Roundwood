# Door/frame assembly mirror: name the four-cell Assembly or freeze the frame copy

Baseline review 2026-10-21 (abstraction lens); widened by the 2026-10-22 assembly plan (artifacts jobs/2026-10-22-roundwood-review/assembly-plan.md).

Problem: every lifecycle rule (retry delay, restoring-snapshot guard, loaded check) existed three times (door, compatibility frame, and since 0.12.0 the archway), plus three anchor lookups, three restyling targets, three placement writers and four copies of anchor-entity persistence.

Done (0.12.0 refactor, behaviour-preserving apart from the rollback below):
- block/assembly/Assembly is the narrow contract (anchor, facing, ordered parts, pos, family isPart) with the shared loaded-safe traversal and authored frame; DoorLayout, ArchwayLayout and TrimLayout implement it, keeping record identities, part enums, membership, stateFor and bounds.
- AssemblyIntegrity and AssemblyRemoval replace DoorIntegrity/Removal, ArchwayIntegrity/Removal and TrimIntegrity/Removal (deleted). The door's camouflage release is the pre-teardown hook (after the guards, before partner collection).
- AssemblyAnchors (family class plus reconstructed layout equality; captured-entity loot fallback) behind the typed DoorBlockEntities and ArchwayBlockEntities facades and TrimAppearance; appearance/AssemblyAppearanceTarget is the one restyling target (clicked-cell origin).
- placement/AssemblyPlacementPlan (shared validation skeleton; typed Door/ArchwayPlacementPlan facades kept), AssemblyInstallation, AssemblyPlacement. The one intended behaviour change: a write refused part-way, a refused batch, a missing anchor entity or an exception rolls every cell back (states and block-entity data, drops and teardown suppressed, only its own block snapshots removed) and the placement fails with no charge or success effects.
- block/attachment/entity/AppearanceAnchorBlockEntity: one look/stores/save/load/update/packet lifecycle for the door, archway, window and frame entities, with family hooks (camouflage, glass, window legacy reading and model refresh).

Frame decision: adopted, not frozen. Its compatibility identity, empty collision, floor-only checks, restyling and migration remain family policy; no player placement returns. Decision recorded in docs/DECISIONS.md.
