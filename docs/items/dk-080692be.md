# FPS drops around showcase items

Owner reports poor FPS around the showcase. Diagnosis at detached 63504c0 (Roundwood 0.16.0, Forge 1.20.1) completed Stages 1-3 without starting a client or implementing a production fix. Actual FPS, a good-build comparison and the owner's exact scene remain unmeasured.

Stage 1 census: 25 arch anchors, 18 door anchors (12 fitted), 85 windows, 97 signs, one barrel. If all pass visibility/completeness, the workload is 215 static tessellations plus 26 leaf draws. Stage 2 replay measured 56,168 quads/224,672 vertices. Fitted frames/casings account for 46,074 quads (82% of all Roundwood quads); their per-pixel surface generator feeds masonry subdivision, then a BER emits the cached quads each eligible frame. Two counting-consumer JVMs measured fitted emission at 25.596/36.011 ms and 7,371,840 allocated B per census pass, excluding AO/world lighting, buffer upload and complete renderer cost. Machine contention limits timing precision.

A diagnostic control using existing merged arch surfaces preserved oriented unit-surface coverage and area by normal/vertex color across all 135 fitted cases. It reduced fitted geometry to 8,994 quads (80.5% fewer), measured emission to 7.570/6.434 ms, and allocations to 1,439,072 B/pass. This is not a shipped fix or visual UV/AO proof. A real headless GameTest replay passed 1/1: assembly construction/completeness approximately 0.197 ms and 73,240 B/pass; bounds approximately 0.064 ms and 30,128 B/pass. Signs were measured only as board geometry plus a glyph-emission proxy, not a complete renderer.

Fix specification: first consolidate fitted surfaces with existing merged geometry; then chunk-bake fitted frames, standalone arches/casings and other static attachments through immutable per-cell ModelData derived from anchor snapshots. Eliminate all 215 census static BER submissions, preserve lifecycle/cross-section invalidation and compatibility, then optimize surviving leaf lookup/assembly/bounds allocations. Closed-leaf baking is conditional on a correct asynchronous chunk/BER ownership handoff; retain leaf BER until that is demonstrated. Acceptance and exact limitations are in the report.
